### Purchasing rules

Separate the agency fee, supplier price, logistics and contingency. Do not fund a client’s print or interiors purchase from monthly retainer cash. Secure the agreed production advance before purchase orders. Obtain written approval of samples, dimensions, quantities, material, installation, lead times and acceptance criteria.

PR is billed for agreed research, material, outreach and reporting; earned coverage is not guaranteed. Paid placements are separately approved and disclosed appropriately. Events need venue, crew, access, rehearsal, contingency and cancellation terms. Staff training is a later pilot, not an unlimited package inclusion.

### Hero media decision

The existing eight-second, 16:9, 360-degree visual idea can be used as an optional supporting media block. Keep it free of zoom effects as requested. Headline and brief explanation first; action next; proof directly below. Work cards stack vertically. Package cards remain readable without sideways swiping. FAQs expand with accessible controls. A mobile contact action must not cover captions, form buttons or browser controls.
